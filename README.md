Poinsettia 4.0 accepts image uploads but does not provide audio import or microphone controls. A standalone edition may preserve this information in local application data when it is updated.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Poinsettia General Suite, including the Poinsettia 2, Poinsettia 3.9, and Poinsettia 4.0 chat experiences, standalone annual editions, the public website, account services, conversation storage, generated-file features, multimodal image and speech/audio input where supported, and authorized update handlers. 02. Information we process

Account and consent information. When you create an account, Poinsettia stores your username, a securely generated password hash rather than your plaintext password, your age confirmation, EULA review and agreement status, the accepted EULA version, Privacy Policy agreement status, the accepted Privacy Policy version, and the relevant consent timestamps.

Content and files. When you use the signed-in application, Poinsettia stores your conversation messages and generated responses so your conversations can be restored across sessions and supported application editions. It also stores uploaded or generated file content, file names, file type, size, and creation metadata when you use the file features. When files, images, or audio are provided with a Poinsettia 3 request, or images are provided with a Poinsettia 4 request, that content is used to help produce the response and is processed by the configured local Ollama runtime. Raw image and audio attachments are not stored as conversation message content by the application.

Standalone application data. A locally installed edition may keep conversation and account data in an application database, such as a file named poinsettia.db, and generated files in a local application-data directory. The exact location depends on the operating system and edition. This local data is user data, not merely temporary installation files.

Sessions and preferences. The application uses signed session cookies to keep you signed in and to maintain a secure session. Selected display preferences, such as font, text size, and sidebar state, may be stored in your browser’s local storage. These browser preferences are not needed to identify you.

Technical information. The hosting and web-server infrastructure may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser or device details, timestamps, and error or security logs to operate and protect the service. When you click the Record Audio or microphone button, your browser may request permission to use your microphone. If you grant permission, Poinsettia records audio from the microphone, converts it for processing, and submits the recording with your Poinsettia 3 request for analysis. Poinsettia does not ask for a real name, telephone number, or payment information to create a free account.

Updates and distribution. If a standalone edition includes an update handler, it may process the installed version, operating-system or compatibility information, update availability, installation status, and download or error status. The Microsoft Store or another authorized distributor may separately process Store account, device, licensing, download, and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy. Poinsettia does not need to inspect the contents of your local chat database merely to check whether an update is available.

04. Poinsettia 3.9 and 4.0 research and multimodal inference

Poinsettia 3.9 and Poinsettia 4.0 can retrieve current information when your request calls for web research, weather, or related data. To do that, relevant query text may be sent to external services and websites, including search and encyclopedia services, OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim geocoding service, the National Weather Service, and source webpages selected for citations.

Those providers may receive the query, technical request information, and the IP address or other information normally disclosed by a web request. They operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not include sensitive personal information in a query that you do not want sent to an external source.

Poinsettia 2, Poinsettia 3.9, and Poinsettia 4.0 inference is served through the project’s configured Ollama runtime. Poinsettia 3.9 uses native image and speech/audio understanding for attached media; Poinsettia 4.0 uses native image understanding only. The application does not intentionally send ordinary prompts or attached media to a separate hosted AI provider, but retrieved third-party content may be included in a Poinsettia 3.9 or Poinsettia 4.0 response request.
