Quick start guide

Getting started with Poinsettia

A simple path from opening the app to getting useful answers, researching current topics, understanding images and audio, and creating files with Poinsettia.

Your first conversation

Sign in or create an account first, then choose the model that fits your task and type your request in the chat box.

01

Open Poinsettia

Select Launch App from the navigation or go directly to the chat experience.

02

Choose a model

Use Poinsettia 2 for everyday conversation, or Poinsettia 3.9 when your question needs current web information or multimodal image/audio understanding.

03

Ask clearly

Describe your goal, include useful context, and mention the format or level of detail you want in the answer.

04

Keep exploring

Continue the conversation with follow-up questions. Switch models any time from the chat header.

Which model should I use?

Poinsettia 2

Best for brainstorming, writing, explanations, coding help, and general conversation without a live web search.

Poinsettia 3.9

Best for current events, recent facts, research, image questions, and speech/audio understanding. It retains citations, weather, and downloadable file creation, and an internet connection is required for live research.

Get better results

Specific requests help Poinsettia understand what a useful answer looks like. Try including your audience, constraints, and preferred format.

Writing

Tell Poinsettia what you are writing and who will read it.

Rewrite this as a concise, friendly email to a new customer.

Research

Ask Poinsettia 3.9 to compare information and show its sources.

Compare the latest approaches and cite the sources you used.

Creating downloadable files

With Poinsettia 3.9 selected, ask it to create a text file. When the response is complete, the generated file appears in the Files panel and can be downloaded later.

Create a .txt file with a one-week study plan for learning Python.

Images and speech/audio

Choose Poinsettia 3.9, then use the image button to attach one or more images. Use the audio button to import a recording, or the microphone button to record speech directly in the browser. Audio is converted to 16 kHz mono WAV before it is sent to Gemma 4 12B's native audio input.

The media is sent to the local Ollama model with your prompt. Raw image and audio bytes are not saved as conversation messages; remove attachments before sending if you do not want them included in the request.

Poinsettia commitment

Free access, built to last

Poinsettia General Suite will never be monetized and will continue to run as free software for the user.

Our free-access policy

Our commitment: Poinsettia General Suite will never be monetized. The service will continue to be available to users as a free-to-use experience without subscriptions, paywalls, or required payments for its core functionality.

This policy reflects Poinsettia’s purpose: making capable AI assistance available to people without putting essential access behind a price tag.

✓

Free to use: Users can access the core Poinsettia experience without paying a subscription or usage fee.

✓

No required upgrade: Poinsettia 2 and Poinsettia 3.9 remain part of the free user experience.

✓

Continued availability: The project is intended to continue operating as a free service for users.

Free to use is not the same as open-source

“Free” in this policy means free of charge for the user. It does not change the software licensing terms described in the documentation.

The Poinsettia General Suite application remains proprietary and closed-source. The Apache License 2.0 applies only to the un-altered baseline model components identified in the licensing documentation.

What this means for users

Start without payment

You can begin using Poinsettia after creating a free account. No subscription or payment information is required.

Use the tools included

Core chat, model switching, Poinsettia 3.9 web research, citations, multimodal input, and downloadable text files remain part of the free experience.
