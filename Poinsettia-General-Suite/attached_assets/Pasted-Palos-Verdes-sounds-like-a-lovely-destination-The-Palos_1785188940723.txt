Palos Verdes sounds like a lovely destination! The Palos Verdes Peninsula is known for its stunning coastline, scenic hiking trails, and beautiful beaches. Here are some tips for planning your trip:

**Getting There:** The nearest airport is Long Beach Airport (LGB), which offers flights from major airlines.

**Accommodations:** Choose from a range of hotels, inns, and vacation rentals in the area. Consider staying in Rolling Hills Estates or Rancho Palos Verdes for easy access to beaches and attractions.

**Must-see Attractions:**

1. **Point Vicente Lighthouse:** Climb to the top of this historic lighthouse for breathtaking views of the Pacific Ocean.
2. **Palos Verdes Peninsula Coastal Trail:** Explore over 25 miles of trails that offer stunning ocean views, scenic lookouts, and access to secluded beaches.
3. **Abalone Gardens:** Stroll through these beautiful gardens, which feature an array of plants, flowers, and trees native to the area.

Now, let's talk about the Computer History Museum (CHM). Located in Mountain View, California, the CHM is a must-visit for tech enthusiasts.

**When to Stop at the Computer History Museum:**

If you're planning a road trip from Los Angeles to San Francisco or vice versa, consider stopping at the CHM on your way. The museum is about 30 miles south of San Jose and 45 miles north of San Francisco, making it a convenient stop along the I-280 corridor.

Here's a rough outline:

* From Los Angeles, take I-5 N to San Jose (approx. 450 miles, 7 hours)
* Stop at the CHM on your way to explore exhibits on computer history, innovation, and technology.
* Continue to San Francisco or your final destination (approx. 45 miles, 1 hour)

If you're driving from San Francisco to Los Angeles, consider stopping at the CHM on your way back.

**Alternative Route:** If you'd rather avoid the crowded I-280 corridor, take US-101 N from San Jose or San Francisco to the CHM.

Keep in mind that traffic and road conditions can affect travel times. Be sure to check for updates before embarking on your trip.
